❤️ Our Mission & Values

Better Food for More People

From a humble restaurant discovery directory founded in 2008 to one of the world's largest food ordering and instant commerce ecosystems.

🌍 Delivering happiness across 1,000+ cities with 300,000+ restaurant partners

Our Core Pillars

We connect diners, restaurants, and delivery partners into a seamless, empowering network.

🍽️

Food Discovery & Delivery

Enabling millions of customers to discover top eateries and receive fresh food quickly right at their doorstep.

🏪

Empowering Restaurants

Providing cutting-edge inventory management, kitchen supplies (Hyperpure), customer analytics, and transparent payments.

🤝

Feeding India Foundation

A non-profit social initiative dedicated to eradicating hunger, serving millions of nutritious daily meals to communities in need.

Our Journey at a Glance

2008

Founded as Foodiebay

Started as a scanned restaurant menu directory in New Delhi to help people find great local food.

2015

Online Ordering Launch

Expanded into direct online ordering and doorstep delivery, building a massive hyperlocal fleet.

2021

Historic Public Listing

Successfully listed on NSE & BSE, becoming India's pioneering tech unicorn to go public.
