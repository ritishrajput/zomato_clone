Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to register my restaurant?

You only need a valid FSSAI registration certificate, PAN Card, GST certificate (if applicable), a cancelled cheque / bank account details, and your restaurant menu.

How long does it take for my restaurant to go live on Zomato?

Once your documents and menu are verified by our onboarding team, your store will go live within 24 to 48 business hours.

Who handles the food delivery?

Zomato’s fleet of dedicated delivery partners will pick up the freshly prepared food from your counter and deliver it directly to customer doors with live GPS tracking.
